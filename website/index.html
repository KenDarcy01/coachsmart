Then the smart stuff

Less admin, more coaching

Once you know who's coming, CoachSmart helps you plan the session, keep the score and write it all up. Just talk to it.

AI · VOICE

Session plans that
write themselves

Pick your favourite drills, set the length and say what you want to focus on. You get a full plan with timings, stations and coaching points, with the weather checked. Want a change? Say it.

  • Build from your favourite drills
  • Change anything by voice
  • Stations that rotate, with timings
  • Share plans with the other coaches
PRINT-READY

In your club's colours

Session plans and drill cards as PDFs with your crest, timings and diagrams. Share them or print them for the clubhouse.

MATCHDAY

Score keeper

The clock runs and you keep the score, by tap or by voice: hold the mic and say "Point, Cian". It keeps working with no signal and keeps the screen awake. At full time it checks the score with you, and the charts and timeline are ready to share.

  • Running clock for each half
  • Tap or voice scoring
  • Works offline, saves as you go
  • Full-time check against the ref's score
LIBRARY

Your club's drill library

Warm-ups, drills and games by age and code, each with a diagram and video. Pick your favourites and build a plan from them.

AI

Add your own drills

Take a photo or sketch it, then describe it by voice. CoachSmart writes it up and draws a clean diagram for your library.

AI

Match reports in your own words

Talk through the game on the drive home. CoachSmart tidies it into a report, keeps how you said it, and saves it with the match.

"Slow start, two points down at the water break, but the subs gave us legs and Ciara's goal settled it."
After a slow start we were two points down at the water break. The subs brought fresh legs and Ciara Nolan's goal settled it in the second half.
Speaks coach

Talk to it like you'd talk to your selector

Every mic in CoachSmart works the same way: hold, speak, check the words, done. It understands GAA terms, keeps your own words and never makes you fill in a form on the sideline.

"Make the warm-up fifteen minutes and take out the cool-down."
Plan updated. Warm-up is now 15 minutes and the cool-down is removed.
