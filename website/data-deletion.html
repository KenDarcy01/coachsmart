DATA DELETION

Last updated: 11 September 2026

You have the right to request deletion of your CoachSmart account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to submit a request, what data will be deleted, and what (if anything) is retained and why.

How to Request Deletion

To delete your account and data, send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your CoachSmart account. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email when deletion is complete.

  1. Send an email to privacy@coachsmart.app from the email address linked to your CoachSmart account.
  2. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line, and your full name in the body of the email.
  3. We will verify your identity and confirm receipt within 5 business days.
  4. Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your confirmed request.
  5. You will receive a final email confirming that deletion is complete.

Submit a deletion request

What Gets Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following personal data associated with your account is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Action
Account credentials (email address, password hash) Deleted
Profile information (name, profile photo) Deleted
Club and team memberships Deleted
Event attendance responses Deleted
Car pool entries linked to your account Deleted
Push notification tokens and notification history Deleted
Squad selections and match reports authored by you Deleted
Any uploaded images linked to your account Deleted

What May Be Retained

In limited circumstances, some data may be retained after your account is deleted. This only occurs where we are legally required to do so.

Data Retention Reason
Payment transaction records (if applicable) 7 years Irish financial and tax law
Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics Indefinite No personal data — cannot be linked back to you

Retained data is held securely and used only for the purpose stated above. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Effect on Club Data

CoachSmart is a team management platform. Deleting your account removes your personal data from our systems, but does not automatically remove club-level data (such as team rosters or event records) that your club administrator manages independently.

If you are an administrator and the only admin for your club, please contact us before requesting deletion so we can discuss how to handle the club's data or transfer admin rights to another member.

Contact Us

For any questions about data deletion, your rights under GDPR, or how we handle your personal data, contact our privacy team.
