AI

Match reports in your own words

Talk through the game on the drive home. CoachSmart tidies it into a report, keeps how you said it, and saves it with the match.

"Slow start, two points down at the water break, but the subs gave us legs and Ciara's goal settled it."
After a slow start we were two points down at the water break. The subs brought fresh legs and Ciara Nolan's goal settled it in the second half.
Speaks coach

Talk to it like you'd talk to your selector

Every mic in CoachSmart works the same way: hold, speak, check the words, done. It understands GAA terms, keeps your own words and never makes you fill in a form on the sideline.

"Make the warm-up fifteen minutes and take out the cool-down."
Plan updated. Warm-up is now 15 minutes and the cool-down is removed.
"Point, Cian."
