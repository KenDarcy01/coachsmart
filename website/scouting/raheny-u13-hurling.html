The short version

  • We play them today.Sat 31 Oct, 1:30pm at St Annes 21, last round of Div 2, with us at home.
  • They haven't won in Div 2.0 from 8 and bottom. Their margins run from 6 to 18 points, by 13 a game on average.
  • They can't kick points.3.5 points a game, less than half the division average. Three times they kicked one point or none.
  • Goals are their only real weapon.18 goals in 8 games, almost exactly our rate. Two thirds of their scoring comes from goals.
  • Their defence leaks goals.Every Div 2 side has scored at least 3 goals against them, and Castleknock scored 3-18.
  • They were good in the spring.Raheny's U13s were 2nd of 6 in the Div 4 grading league (3 wins, 2 defeats). Div 2 has been a much bigger test.

How to beat them

  • Shut the goal door.Goals are 66% of their scoring and we concede 5.1 goals a game, the most in Div 2. That's the one way they can stay in it. Keep the full-back line tight and don't let runners through one on one.
  • Make them shoot from distance.They've kicked 3.5 points a game and never more than 7. If their only option is a point, they won't keep up.
  • Keep kicking our points.We average 9.6 points a game and teams kick 10.8 against them. Taking our points steadily should be enough on its own.
  • Go for goals too.They concede 4.3 goals a game and nobody has scored fewer than 3. Our 2.4 goals a game should rise against them.
  • Use the common form.We beat Naomh Barrog by 5; Raheny lost to them by 14. Against the eight sides we've both played, our average margin is 4 points a game better.
