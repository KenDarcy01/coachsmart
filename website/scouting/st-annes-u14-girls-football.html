The short version

  • Shield final on Sun 25 Oct.Listed for 1pm at Boharnabrenna, their home pitch. They beat St Brigids 2-6 to 1-6 in their semi-final; we beat Ballyboden 4-8 to 2-5 in ours.
  • They've won every game at home.4 from 4 at Boharnabrenna, by 9, 55, 24 and 3 points. Away they've won 2 of 6, and the four defeats were all away.
  • They came up from Div 7.In the spring they were 2nd in Div 7, level on points with Na Dubhgall, and lost the playoff final to them by 6. In the autumn they were put into the Div 6 championship, where we played Div 6 all year.
  • Na Dubhgall have their number, nobody else does.Three of their four defeats were to Na Dubhgall, by 1, 6 and 1. The other was by 15 away to Scoil Ui Chonaill, who went on to top the group.
  • Goals, goals, goals.58 goals in 10 games, nearly 6 a game, and 67% of their scoring comes from goals. They scored 16-14 against Good Counsel and 7-7 against Ballyboden.
  • They can be scored on.37 goals conceded, 3.7 a game. Scoil Ui Chonaill put 6-12 past them and Na Dubhgall scored 7 goals in the spring league game.
  • Tight games go either way.They've been in four games decided by 6 or less: won by 3 (St Brigids), lost by 1 twice and by 6, all to Na Dubhgall.

Margin game by game

St Annes winSt Annes loss

Spring league and playoff on the left, then the Div 6 championship and the shield semi-final. Their biggest win was 55 points at home to Good Counsel, who lost every game. Their only heavy defeat was away to Scoil Ui Chonaill in September.

Scored v conceded

St Annes scoredConceded

In Div 7 they scored 33 a game and conceded 14. In the Div 6 championship they scored 22 a game and conceded 19, so the step up cost them about 10 points a game at each end. They scored only 12 in the shield semi-final, their lowest of the year.

Goals and points

Two thirds of their scoring is goals. Their point-taking is modest, 8.5 a game, so if the goal chances are cut off they don't have a lot else. At the other end they concede goals and points in about the same mix as they score them.
