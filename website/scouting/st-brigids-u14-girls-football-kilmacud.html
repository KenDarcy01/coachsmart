The short version

  • Shield final on Sun 25 Oct.It's listed for 1pm at Russell Park, their home ground. We got there by beating Clontarf 7-5 to 3-10 on Wednesday, our first win since May.
  • They've beaten us twice this year.2-9 to 1-3 at Russell Park in April, and 1-10 to 2-5 at Pairc De Burca in August. The August game was lost by 2, and they kicked 10 points to our 5.
  • They won the spring league without dropping a point.7 from 7 in Div 1, including a walkover from Castleknock, and a scoring difference of +77. We finished 6th.
  • They're beatable now.After beating us in the championship group they lost at home to Ballyboden by 2 and Castleknock by 9. Both defeats came at Russell Park, where the final is.
  • They don't concede much.11.3 points a game against, the best in Div 1 in the spring, and only 15 goals in 10 games. Ballinteer's 1-10 is the most points anyone has kicked against them.
  • Their scoring dropped in the autumn.22 points a game in the spring league, then 13, 12 and 6 in the three championship games. They bounced back with 7-10 against Cuala in the shield semi-final.
  • They kick points.Only 57% of their scoring comes from goals, against 65% for us. Keeping the game tight won't be enough on its own; they'll keep taking points from play.

Scored v conceded

St Brigids scoredConceded

In the spring they scored 22 a game and conceded 9. In the championship group they conceded a little more, 13 a game, but scored only 10. Castleknock held them to 1-3, their lowest total of the year.

Goals and points

They kick more points than most teams at this age: 83 points to 37 goals. We're the other way round, with 65% of our scores coming from goals. At the other end they concede 1.5 goals a game, and we scored 2 goals in each of our games against them.
