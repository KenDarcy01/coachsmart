The short version

  • It's Na Fianna in the final.Na Fianna beat Kilmacud Crokes 4-6 to 0-1 in yesterday's semi-final, and we beat Lucan 1-2 to 0-4. Sportlomo doesn't list a date for the final yet.
  • One defeat all year.They've won 10 of 11, including a walkover. The only loss was 1-12 to 0-4 away to Scoil Ui Chonaill in April. Scoil went unbeaten and won the spring league, and Na Fianna finished second.
  • Goals win their games.60 goals in 10 games, so 73% of their scoring comes from goals. The Scoil game is the only one where they didn't score a goal, and it's the only game they lost.
  • Hardly anyone scores against them.They concede 5.8 points a game. In the championship they've let in 12 points in four games. Only Round Tower and we have scored a goal against them.
  • We ran them closest.They won 3-5 to 2-1 at our place on 3 Oct, by 7 points. That's their smallest championship margin, and 14 points is their lowest championship score.
  • Stronger at home.They've won all five home games by an average of 22. Away the average is 15 and includes the Scoil loss. The final venue isn't known yet.
