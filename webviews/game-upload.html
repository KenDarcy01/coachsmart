How to draw your diagram
Circles
Numbered circle = Player
Side by side, numbers sum to 15 → opponents (3 & 12, 6 & 9…)
Side by side, same number → drill opponents
Single team spread out → all red
Circle with C = Coach
Renders as a black circle
Empty circle = Football
Small circle, no label
Other shapes
Triangle = Cone
Renders as an orange cone
H shape = Goalposts
Two uprights and a crossbar
Lines & arrows
Solid arrow = Player run
Straight, curved, or wavy
Dashed arrow = Kick / Pass
Renders in yellow
Pitch line = Zone boundary
13m, 20m, 45m, halfway — long line spanning full width, no arrowhead
Arc = Pitch arc
D-arc, 2-pointer arc, centre circle — curved pitch markings, no arrowhead
Tips for best results
• Keep circles clearly round and label them legibly
• Use numbered circles for players — matchstick figures won't be recognised
• Opponent pairs: draw side by side. Numbers that sum to 15 (e.g. 3 & 12) are automatically coloured red vs blue
• Draw arrows that clearly start and end at shapes
• Pitch zone lines should span the full width — the AI tells them apart from movement arrows
