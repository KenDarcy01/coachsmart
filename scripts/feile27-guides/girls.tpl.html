Four golden rules

Easy means easy

Run at chat pace: you can talk in full sentences. Walking for a bit is fine.

Weak side twice

Whatever you do with your good side, do it twice with the other one.

Tick it off

Open the session, tick each exercise, and press Watch if you're not sure how.

Rest is training too

Missed a day? Don't catch up. Just do today's plan.
