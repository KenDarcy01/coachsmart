The four parts

What each one does, where it lives and who sees it

1

{{i:calendar-days}}Dates and timeline

Claire's coaches' timeline is the master plan. Its dates go into one key dates sheet, and the key dates page reads that sheet live: change a date once and every countdown and timeline follows.

Féile is two weekends, and both get the same weight everywhere: football on Sat 10 April and camogie on Sat 8 May 2027.

2

{{i:person-running}}Physical prep

Team training on Monday (football) and Wednesday (camogie), group S&C on Tuesday and matches on Sundays. Around that, short home sessions, two easy runs and daily touches keep everyone moving when pitches are scarce, right through Christmas.

The plan builds in phases to each weekend, with an easier spell before each one so the girls arrive fresh. Fuel for Féile covers eating and drinking well for energy, with a phone poster and a fridge poster.

3

{{i:seedling}}Player development

Every girl gets a superpower and one or two things she's working on next, in football and in camogie. Coaches check in every fortnight from 27 January to 24 March, using the check-ins portal, and each girl gets her own card with tips and videos.

Development groups add a few extra exercises on home nights, with separate groups for football and camogie. A girl picks her groups herself in the diary. There are no accounts, and the public diary never shows who is in which group.

4

{{i:bullhorn}}Reaching families

The Féile pack is the one link families need. Every guide, poster and set of dates goes in it, each with a ready-made WhatsApp message, so nothing gets lost in a group chat.

The parents' guide explains how the whole programme works. The girls' guide is the short version: your week, four golden rules and what to do every day.

Keeping it all together

Small habits that keep every page right without extra work

{{i:mobile-screen-button}}

Every session in CoachSmart

Put trainings and matches in CoachSmart as usual. The diary updates itself, so the girls always see the real week.

{{i:clipboard-check}}

Check-ins every fortnight

A few minutes per girl in the portal: her superpower, what she's working on next and a video to help.

{{i:calendar-days}}

Change a date once

Update it in the key dates sheet. The dates page and countdowns follow, and the pack posters get refreshed.

{{i:box-open}}

Send families to the pack

New material goes into the pack, not as a loose attachment. One link to share, always the latest.

{{i:shield-heart}}

Positive words, always

Everything for the girls talks about skills, energy and the ability to last a full match. Always upbeat, always about what she can do.

{{i:lock}}

Names stay private

No girl's name or group appears on a public page. Each family sees only their own girl's card.
