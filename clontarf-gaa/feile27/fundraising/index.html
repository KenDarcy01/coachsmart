    How you can help

    Every family doing a little gets us there.

    • Take partBuy a 150 Club number or hamper raffle tickets, and come along to the variety night.
    • Ask aroundFamily, neighbours and workmates are often happy to take a ticket or sponsor the walk.
    • Know a business?A local business, or your own workplace, might sponsor the girls. Pass the name to a coach.
    • Give an hourBag packs, the walk and the variety night all need a few parents on the day.
    • Donate a prizeHamper items and raffle prizes all help, big or small.
    • Spread the wordShare the dates with family and friends so nobody misses a draw or the big night.
    Paying in. The coaches will share how to pay for tickets, numbers and sponsorship when each one launches. If you're unsure about anything, ask a coach.
